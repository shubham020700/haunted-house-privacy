Privacy Policy for Haunted House Ghost Guard

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Haunted House Ghost Guard ("the game") is a ghost-protector action game published by Shubham Pandey.

Summary

The game works fully offline, and the online features are optional. When you use them, the game connects to Google Firebase to keep a leaderboard, an optional cloud backup of your progress and, if you choose, a link to your Google account. We do not sell your data, show third-party ads or use analytics or advertising SDKs. There is no chat and no way to type text that other players see.

What is collected, and why

  • An anonymous account ID created automatically by Firebase Authentication, so scores and your backup belong to you. It does not contain your name.
  • Weekly challenge scores (score, stars, kills, time), stored with a display name that the game generates from a fixed word list, for example "Pale Wisp 42". You never type a name, and it is not your real name.
  • Cloud backup of your progress (levels cleared, currency, upgrades, settings), stored so you can restore it on a new phone.
  • If you tap Sign in with Google: your Google account ID and email address, provided by Google, are attached to the same account so that your progress follows you. We use them only for this.
  • Technical data (such as an installation ID and IP address) that Firebase processes to run the service and protect it from abuse.

Nothing else is collected. We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, location or files.

Where it is stored and who processes it

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Authentication, Cloud Functions and Remote Config), a service provider acting for us. It is encrypted in transit. It is kept until you ask us to delete it. Google's own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices and deleting your data

You can play without any online features. In the game, open the online panel (cloud button) and tap Delete my online data to erase your account, scores and backup immediately. You can also email us. To have your account, scores and backup deleted, email spandey020700@gmail.com from the address on your Google account (or tell us your display name). We delete the data within 30 days. You can also sign out of Google in the online panel at any time.

Data on your device

Your progress is also saved on your device using Android's local app storage. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage removes the local copy; the cloud copy stays until you ask for it to be deleted.

Permissions

The game uses the Android Internet permission, to reach Firebase. It requests no other permissions.

Links to other apps

The home screen shows a banner for our other game, Aura Leveling. Tapping it opens that game's Google Play page in the Play Store or your browser. The Play Store and our other game have their own privacy policies: Google Play (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Aura Leveling (https://shubham020700.github.io/aurafitness-privacy/).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used the online features, email us and we will delete the data.
