Privacy Policy for Haunted House Ghost Guard

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Haunted House Ghost Guard ("the game") is a ghost-protector action game published by Shubham Pandey.

Summary

The game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no in-game chat.

Data on your device

Your progress (levels cleared, currency, upgrades, settings and daily rewards) is saved only on your device using Android's local app storage. It is never uploaded. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

Permissions

The game requests no Android permissions. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

Links to other apps

The home screen shows a banner for our other game, Aura Leveling. Tapping it opens that game's Google Play page in the Play Store or your browser. The Play Store and our other game have their own privacy policies: Google Play (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Aura Leveling (https://shubham020700.github.io/aurafitness-privacy/).

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.
