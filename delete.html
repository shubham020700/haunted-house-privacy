Delete your data: Haunted House Ghost Guard

This page explains how to delete your account and data in Haunted House Ghost Guard (package com.aura.hauntedghostguard), published by Shubham Pandey.

Option 1: in the game (instant)

  1. Open the game and tap the cloud button on the home screen.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the online panel and tap Delete my online data.
  3. Confirm. Your data is erased straight away.

Option 2: by email

Email spandey020700@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Haunted House Ghost Guard data". Send it from the Google account you signed in with, or include your generated display name (for example "Pale Wisp 42", shown in the online panel). We delete the data within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous online account and the link to your Google account (including the email address provided by Google sign-in).
  • Your cloud backup of progress.
  • Your weekly challenge scores and leaderboard entries.
  • Your generated display name and any uncollected prizes.

What is kept

Nothing about you is kept on our servers after deletion, and we keep no backup copies. Progress saved on your own phone is not affected; uninstall the game or clear its storage to remove it. Google may keep its own security logs under Google's privacy policy.
